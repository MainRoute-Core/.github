# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

At MainRoute Core, we believe in engineering the future through innovation, collaboration, and community.

We, as contributors, maintainers, project leaders, collaborators, and community members, pledge to make participation in the MainRoute Core ecosystem a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We are committed to providing a positive environment regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, nationality, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, religion, personal appearance, educational background, technical experience, or socioeconomic status.

Our community is built on mutual respect, professional integrity, technical excellence, and a shared passion for creating meaningful technology.

We believe that great engineering is achieved through open collaboration, responsible development, and the collective contributions of people with different perspectives and experiences.

## Our Standards

We expect all members of the MainRoute Core community to demonstrate the following values.

### Positive Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a healthy community include:

- **Respect and Professionalism:** Treat everyone with respect, kindness, and professionalism.
- **Technical Excellence:** Encourage clean code, maintainable architecture, documentation, testing, and constructive engineering practices.
- **Open Collaboration:** Welcome new contributors, different perspectives, and innovative ideas.
- **Constructive Feedback:** Provide helpful, respectful, and technically meaningful feedback.
- **Knowledge Sharing:** Support developers of all experience levels through guidance, documentation, and collaboration.
- **Accountability:** Accept responsibility for mistakes, acknowledge their impact, and learn from them.
- **Community First:** Prioritize the long-term health and success of the open-source ecosystem.
- **Security Awareness:** Respect responsible disclosure practices and avoid exposing sensitive information.
- **Innovation:** Encourage creative problem-solving, experimentation, and responsible technological advancement.
- **Transparency:** Communicate honestly about contributions, project decisions, limitations, and technical challenges.

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, bullying, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting comments, or deliberately disruptive discussions.
- Sexualized language, unwanted advances, or inappropriate conduct.
- Publishing private or confidential information without authorization.
- Deliberately disrupting repositories, discussions, development workflows, or community services.
- Submitting malicious code, intentionally harmful contributions, or unauthorized changes.
- Misrepresenting project ownership, maintainership, affiliations, or official endorsements.
- Plagiarism, impersonation, or knowingly violating third-party intellectual property rights.
- Sharing credentials, API keys, private tokens, or confidential project information.
- Using community platforms for spam, malicious activities, or unauthorized commercial promotion.
- Threatening, intimidating, or retaliating against individuals who report misconduct.
- Any other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional engineering community.

## Engineering and Contribution Principles

MainRoute Core values responsible engineering, technical innovation, and sustainable open-source development.

All contributors are encouraged to:

1. Follow the contribution guidelines and repository-specific requirements.
2. Submit meaningful, documented, tested, and maintainable contributions.
3. Respect existing architecture, project decisions, and technical discussions.
4. Avoid introducing unnecessary complexity or breaking changes without proper discussion.
5. Respect software licenses, copyright, and third-party attribution requirements.
6. Report security vulnerabilities responsibly rather than publicly exposing exploitable details.
7. Respect the work, time, and intellectual contributions of other developers.
8. Avoid using community resources for spam, malicious activities, or unauthorized commercial promotion.
9. Follow established Git workflows and repository maintenance practices.
10. Prioritize accessibility, performance, reliability, and long-term maintainability.
11. Document significant changes and communicate potential compatibility issues.
12. Avoid committing sensitive information, credentials, or private configuration data.

Project-specific contribution requirements may provide additional guidance.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all MainRoute Core community spaces, including but not limited to:

- Official GitHub organizations and repositories.
- Issues, pull requests, discussions, and code reviews.
- Community communication channels and collaboration platforms.
- Official websites and social media channels.
- Open-source projects maintained under MainRoute Core.
- Online and offline events where individuals represent MainRoute Core.
- Other platforms and services officially operated or maintained by MainRoute Core.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing MainRoute Core in public or professional settings.

Examples of representation include using an official email address, posting through official social media accounts, or acting as an appointed representative at an event.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers and community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

They have the authority to take fair and appropriate corrective action in response to conduct that is inappropriate, harmful, threatening, or disruptive.

Depending on the circumstances, maintainers may:

- Remove or edit inappropriate comments and contributions.
- Reject pull requests or other submissions that violate community standards.
- Restrict participation in discussions or community spaces.
- Temporarily suspend contributor privileges.
- Permanently remove individuals from community spaces.
- Report serious security or legal concerns through appropriate channels.

Moderation decisions should be handled fairly, consistently, and with appropriate consideration of the circumstances.

Community leaders should communicate the reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate while respecting the privacy of everyone involved.

## Reporting Guidelines

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the MainRoute Core team.

**Official reporting contact:** tellus@mrc.com

Please include the following information when possible:

- A description of the incident.
- Relevant dates and locations.
- Links, screenshots, or other supporting evidence.
- The usernames or accounts involved.
- Any additional context that may help the team understand the situation.

All reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as possible.

The MainRoute Core team will respect the privacy of individuals reporting incidents and will handle reports confidentially to the extent reasonably possible.

Retaliation against individuals who report concerns in good faith is not acceptable.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will consider the nature, severity, frequency, and impact of violations when determining appropriate consequences.

### 1. Correction

**Community Impact:** Minor inappropriate behavior, unprofessional language, or conduct that makes community participation uncomfortable.

**Consequence:** A private written warning explaining the violation, identifying its impact, and requesting appropriate behavioral changes.

### 2. Warning

**Community Impact:** A significant violation or repeated inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:** An official warning with clear expectations for future conduct. Further violations may result in temporary or permanent restrictions.

### 3. Temporary Suspension

**Community Impact:** Serious misconduct, repeated violations, harassment, or disruptive behavior.

**Consequence:** Temporary suspension from participation in relevant MainRoute Core community spaces for a specified period.

### 4. Permanent Ban

**Community Impact:** A sustained pattern of violations, severe harassment, threats, malicious conduct, or serious violations of community safety.

**Consequence:** Permanent removal from relevant MainRoute Core community spaces and revocation of associated participation privileges.

Community leaders reserve the right to choose an appropriate response based on the severity of an incident.

Serious violations may warrant immediate action without progressing through every enforcement stage.

## Appeals

Individuals who believe an enforcement decision was made unfairly may contact the MainRoute Core team at:

**tellus@mrc.com**

Appeals should include relevant context or additional information that may help clarify the situation.

The team will review appeals fairly, subject to the availability of an appropriate reviewer.

An appeal does not automatically reverse or suspend an existing enforcement decision.

## Our Commitment

MainRoute Core is built on the belief that great technology comes from great collaboration.

We encourage curiosity, creativity, responsible engineering, open knowledge, and meaningful contributions.

Whether you are submitting your first pull request, maintaining a major project, reporting an issue, or sharing an innovative idea, you are part of a community working toward a better technological future.

Our commitment extends beyond writing code. It includes building trust, respecting different perspectives, protecting our community, and creating technology that makes a meaningful difference.

**Be respectful. Build responsibly. Collaborate openly. Engineer the future.**
